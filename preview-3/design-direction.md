# Manav Rachna homepage — Preview 3

## Direction

“Academic command centre.” The third concept replaces the current site’s dark video-first carousel language and the earlier previews’ card-heavy editorial approach with a decisive split-screen entry point, a high-utility programme finder and evidence-led bands. The visual system keeps Manav Rachna’s navy, red and gold, while using sharper grids, restrained corners, strong typographic contrast and purposeful motion.

## Reference response

- Keeps the current site’s recognisable institutional palette, admissions urgency and authentic campus content.
- Applies the brief’s proposed audience journey: focused navigation, discovery, institutions, research, outcomes, campus life, updates and conversion.
- Adopts the reference strengths described in the supplied analysis: early programme discovery, editorial whitespace, prominent outcomes and clear international/research evidence.
- Avoids the current UI’s carousel dependence, competing floating prompts and repeated card systems.

## Distinctive choices

1. Split-screen hero: campus life and the admissions proposition share equal prominence.
2. Numbered journey system: sections and quick paths feel navigable rather than promotional.
3. Programme discovery surface: search and discipline choices appear immediately after the first viewport.
4. Institution switcher: five institutions share one focused comparison surface.
5. Outcome bands: placements and research evidence use high-contrast modular compositions instead of sliders.
6. Responsive mobile flow: the two-dimensional desktop grid collapses into a clear single-column journey with no horizontal dependence.
