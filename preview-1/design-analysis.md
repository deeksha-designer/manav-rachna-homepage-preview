# Manav Rachna homepage redesign — analysis and information architecture

## Current homepage findings

- The present site has strong institutional proof—multiple institutions, research, sports, placements, international ties and a broad academic portfolio—but the story is fragmented across many sliders and repeated card systems.
- The navigation contains valuable depth, but the quantity of top-level labels and long submenu lists makes orientation difficult. The redesign groups this material into six audience-friendly pillars.
- Admission calls to action are visible, but multiple floating prompts and banners compete with the core academic journey.
- The brand is recognisable through deep plum, red and gold. The redesign preserves that equity while introducing calmer surfaces, stronger typography and more deliberate spacing.

## Reference extraction

### Amrita

- Strong interest-led program discovery directly after the hero.
- Editorial typography, generous white space and modular proof sections.
- A compact primary navigation supported by a rich mega menu.
- Rankings and research appear as meaningful evidence rather than isolated badges.

### Chandigarh University

- Admissions and placement outcomes are unmistakable.
- High-impact metrics communicate scale quickly.
- Campus, international experience and career outcomes are treated as decisive student considerations.
- The current reference is information-rich but visually busy; this concept borrows the clarity of its proof points without reproducing the density or persistent promotional overlays.

## Proposed homepage journey

1. Announcement and focused global navigation
2. Brand hero with one primary admissions journey
3. Task-based quick links
4. Interest-led program finder with degree-level tabs
5. Institution selector
6. Research and impact evidence
7. Placement outcomes and student story
8. News, events and campus life
9. Admissions conversion panel
10. Structured footer

## Visual thesis

“Editorial confidence, grounded in outcomes.” Deep plum provides institutional authority; Manav Rachna red drives actions; restrained gold marks distinction. A wide original campus image, large modern typography, line icons and evidence-focused cards replace the carousel-heavy visual language. The page uses generous rhythm, limited radii and minimal animation to feel current without losing academic credibility.

## Scope note

This is intentionally a single-page homepage concept. Navigation items demonstrate the proposed architecture but do not create internal pages.
