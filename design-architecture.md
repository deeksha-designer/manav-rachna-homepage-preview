# Manav Rachna Homepage — Design & Information Architecture

## Design direction

An image-led editorial university homepage that feels established rather than templated. The revised composition follows Amrita's immersive, rounded media framing and dense information architecture while retaining Manav Rachna's own navy, red, white, black and neutral-gray palette, Roboto typography, authentic campus video and original MR imagery. The desktop content system is capped at 1536px and collapses fluidly for tablet and mobile.

## Primary journeys

1. Prospective students: programme search → eligibility and scholarships → application.
2. Parents: institutions → outcomes and placements → enquiries.
3. Researchers: research clusters → centres → collaboration and funding.
4. Current students: notices → ERP and support → events.
5. Partners and alumni: placements and corporate network → news → contact.

## Homepage architecture

1. Utility navigation and admissions alert
2. Main header with six detailed mega-navigation groups
3. Rounded, near-full-bleed campus video hero with floating navigation and contextual promotion card
4. Moving institutional statistics
5. Tabbed notices and important dates
6. Programme search
7. Academic programme levels
8. All five Manav Rachna institutions with dedicated CTAs
9. Admissions journey, support routes and conversion CTAs
10. Research and innovation
11. Sports @ MR using uncropped original imagery
12. Placement achievers, outcome statistics and institutional placement CTAs
13. Corporate network
14. About, institutional statistics and knowledge partners
15. News, stories and events
16. Deep Amrita-inspired information-grid footer and campus enquiry information
17. Persistent quick-action dock

## Navigation taxonomy

- Academics: study level, discipline, online education, programme discovery
- Institutions: MRIIRS, MRU, MRDC, MR Online, MRIS
- Admissions: apply, eligibility, fees, scholarships, loans, international admissions
- Research: clusters, global initiatives, centres, incubation, funding, journals
- Campus Life: sport, clubs, events, support, tour, directions
- About: story, leadership, rankings, accreditation, governance, careers, giving

## Responsive behaviour

- Desktop: 1536px container, hero-overlay navigation, asymmetric editorial grids and fixed quick-action dock.
- Tablet: condensed header, two-column storytelling and reflowed research and events.
- Mobile: single-column admissions and search, swipeable institutions and achievers, bottom quick-action bar, uncropped sports images and compact navigation.

## Media integrity

The hero video, campus imagery, research visual, sports photographs, achiever portraits, recruiter marks and event imagery are sourced from the current Manav Rachna website and stored locally for a dependable preview. Sports imagery preserves natural aspect ratios with `object-fit: contain`. No generated imagery is used in the revised design.
